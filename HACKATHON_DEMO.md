# TrustMesh — 90-second judge demo

**0–15 sec:** Open Mission. Say: “TrustMesh is the zero-trust security layer between autonomous browser agents and the open web.”

**15–35 sec:** Launch the swarm. Show Scout, Verifier, and Adversary as isolated research roles. Explain that none can authorize execution.

**35–55 sec:** Open SignalShield. Show manipulation findings, quarantine, and optionally scan a source with VirusTotal.

**55–70 sec:** Open Evidence. Click one of the source links and show that every conclusion has explicit provenance instead of an AI-generated citation with no URL.

**70–90 sec:** Return to Mission. Show the explainable Trust Score and the locked Executor. Approve only after the human checkpoint.

Closing line: **“Don’t trust your browser agent. Verify it.”**
